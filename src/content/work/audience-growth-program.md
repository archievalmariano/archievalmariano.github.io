## Context

The publication had spent two years optimizing for reach. Traffic was up, but
loyalty was flat and subscription growth had stalled. The question wasn't how to
get more visitors — it was how to turn the ones already arriving into readers who
came back.

## What I did

- Rebuilt the audience dashboard around returning-reader and habit metrics
  rather than pageviews.
- Ran a segmentation of the existing audience to find the "almost loyal" cohort
  most likely to convert.
- Designed a lightweight newsletter program aimed at that cohort, with editorial
  ownership rather than a marketing hand-off.

## Outcome

Within two quarters, returning-reader frequency rose meaningfully and the
newsletter became the single largest driver of subscription starts. More
importantly, the newsroom began making editorial decisions with audience context
built in rather than bolted on.
