## Context

The newsroom had access to more data than ever and trusted almost none of it.
Editors ignored the dashboards; the audience team felt unheard.

## What I did

- Cut the reported metrics down to a handful tied directly to editorial goals.
- Wrote plain-language definitions for each and put them where editors worked.
- Ran short, recurring sessions reading the numbers together rather than sending
  reports into the void.

## Outcome

Analytics stopped being a separate function and became a shared language.
Editorial meetings started referencing audience signals unprompted — the surest
sign the work had landed.
