overview: An existing short-form affiliate program was already producing a steady stream of commerce-led videos across TikTok, Reels, and YouTube Shorts. I was brought in to develop an editorial layer for the channels, creating useful technology and consumer content that could give audiences a reason to follow beyond product promotions alone.
role: With a background in editorial and digital publishing, I was responsible for developing the editorial side of the program. My scope covered content strategy, topic development, and scripting. A separate affiliate team continued to manage production, shoots, publishing, and the broader commerce operation.
context:
  - The affiliate program already had established channel identities, dozens of published videos, and a growing commerce operation.
  - The next challenge was content balance. If every post was primarily transactional, the channels risked becoming little more than sales feeds.
  - Management wanted to introduce more editorial value into the mix, particularly around technology and consumer topics, while keeping the content compatible with a short-form social environment.
approach:
  - Developed a framework for recurring editorial content alongside affiliate-led posts
  - Built a planning sheet combining evergreen topic ideas with time-sensitive technology moments such as major product launches
  - Created more than 50 potential editorial topics for future development
  - Wrote the first 20 short-form scripts to establish tone, structure, and repeatable content patterns
  - Designed the material for use across TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts
outcome: The project left the affiliate team with a ready-to-use editorial framework, more than 50 developed topic ideas, and 20 starter scripts that could serve as the first production batch and a reference point for future content development.
