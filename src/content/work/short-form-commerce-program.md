overview: An existing short-form affiliate program was already producing a steady stream of commerce-led videos across TikTok, Reels, and YouTube Shorts. I was brought in to give the channels an editorial voice, creating useful technology, consumer, and lifestyle content that gave audiences a reason to follow beyond product promotions alone. An initial commission for 20 scripts led to a request for another 20. I chose to leave the team with 50 additional scripts instead, bringing the completed total to 70.
role: With a background in editorial and digital publishing, I developed the editorial side of the program. My scope covered content strategy, topic development, and scripting. A separate affiliate team continued to manage production, shoots, publishing, and the broader commerce operation.
context:
  - The affiliate program already had established channel identities, dozens of published videos, and a growing commerce operation.
  - The next challenge was content balance. If every post was primarily transactional, the channels risked becoming little more than sales feeds.
  - Management wanted to introduce more editorial value into the mix while keeping the content light, useful, and compatible with a short-form social environment.
  - The subject range expanded beyond technology and consumer explainers into everyday lifestyle and local-interest ideas.
approach:
  - Developed a framework for recurring editorial content alongside affiliate-led posts
  - Built a planning sheet combining evergreen ideas, time-sensitive technology moments, consumer guidance, and local lifestyle topics
  - Established a concise, conversational script structure that could carry useful information without feeling like a compressed article
  - Wrote 70 completed scripts across the growing topic range
  - Designed the material for use across TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts
  - Kept the editorial material distinct from direct product promotion while making it suitable for channels built around commerce
outcome: The project left the affiliate team with a reusable editorial framework and 70 completed scripts that could support production and guide future content development. The work grew from an initial batch of 20 to 50 additional scripts, exceeding the follow-up request and leaving a larger bank of material before my departure.
