## Context

Six newsletters had accumulated over the years with overlapping audiences, no
shared standards, and unclear ownership. Open rates were declining and no one
could say what each product was for.

## What I did

- Audited every newsletter against a single question: what is the reader promise?
- Consolidated and retired products that no longer had one.
- Paired each remaining newsletter with a newsroom owner and a small set of
  metrics they actually cared about.

## Outcome

The portfolio shrank but strengthened. Open and click rates recovered, and the
newsletters became a deliberate part of editorial planning rather than an
afterthought.
